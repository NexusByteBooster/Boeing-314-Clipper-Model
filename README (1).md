# Boeing 314 Clipper – 3D Model

A detailed 3D recreation of the Boeing 314 Clipper, one of the most iconic flying boats ever built.

> **Status:** Early development – reference setup and blocking out in progress.

---

## Devlog #1 – Reference Setup & Blocking Out

Over the past few hours, I started work on a detailed 3D model of the Boeing 314 Clipper. Before modeling, I gathered reference material, including top, side, and front-view blueprints, to keep the proportions and dimensions as accurate as possible.

The first stage focused on setting up the reference images and blocking out the aircraft's overall shape. The Boeing 314 has a very distinctive design, with a large boat-shaped fuselage, long tapered wings, and four radial engines, so getting the basic proportions right is one of the most important parts of the project.

The top-view blueprint is currently the primary reference. It helps with accurately positioning the wings, engine nacelles, cockpit, and tail assembly while keeping the aircraft's original geometry.

![Top-view blueprint](images/top-view-blueprint.png)

## Project Goal

Produce a faithful digital recreation of the Boeing 314 Clipper while learning more about its engineering and historical design. Establishing accurate reference geometry now will make the later detailing and texturing process much smoother.
